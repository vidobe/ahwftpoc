Overview

Plan, assign, and execute work from one place.

Build a marketing Operating System by centralizing and integrating work across teams and applications with the industry-leading enterprise marketing work management application.

In this POC, you will have the opportunity to explore Adobe Workfront – the backbone of our GenStudio solution - and try out:

  • Enterprise Planning
  • Centralized Work Management
  • Reviews & Approvals
  • Reporting & Insights

You will also be able to experience first hand the Adobe GenStudio overarching Solution.

This will guide you to explore the integrations with various Adobe products as part of this POC, including GenStudio for Performance Marketing that has a chapter of its own.

Adobe Workfront is a powerful project management and collaboration tool that streamlines your content production workflows, ensuring efficient teamwork and timely delivery.

This POC revolves around automation of your workflows and Transcreation whilst leveraging the already available AI features where they serve the workflows.

We are an innovative company and we are developing on AI (and other areas) at a fast pace. Therefore, we need to separate the mid-term Vision from the POC experience.

Quick Tip

How to use this guide?

Each chapter has a prefix indicating if the chapter is for ADMIN use or for USER use. Depending on your role in the POC, you will be able to go to the chapters that matter to you.

Prerequisites

Access to Adobe Workfront.
